## What I'm Building

### [WooEasyLife](https://app.wpsalehub.com/en) — E-commerce operations & automation

**WooEasyLife** is a product from **WPSaleHub** built for WooCommerce and Facebook-based cash-on-delivery (COD) sellers in Bangladesh. It brings essential order-management tasks into a more connected workflow.

- **Order verification:** Courier delivery-history checks and risk signals across Pathao, Steadfast, and RedX.
- **Checkout protection:** OTP verification, duplicate-order controls, and customer blacklisting.
- **Operations automation:** Courier parcel entry, multi-store workflows, and missing-order recovery.
- **Seller tools:** Return-loss and courier-charge calculators, plus support for managing Facebook Messenger leads from WordPress.

**Explore:** [Live product](https://app.wpsalehub.com/en) · [What we're building](https://app.wpsalehub.com/en/about) · [Messenger workflow](https://app.wpsalehub.com/en/woocommerce-facebook-messenger)
